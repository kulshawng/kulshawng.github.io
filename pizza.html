How to make a pepperoni pizza

‹li> Pizza Dough: 16 oz store-bought or homemade pizza dough (or flour, yeast, water, olive oil, and salt) ‹li> Tomato Sauce: z cup pizza sauce or passata
  • Cheese: 1 ½ to 2 cups freshly shredded mozzarella cheese
    • ﻿﻿Pepperoni: 18 to 20 slices of pepperoni ﻿﻿Toppings & Finish: Olive oil and a very strong recommendation of fresh oregano or hot honey.

    ﻿﻿﻿Preheat the Oven: Heat your oven to 500°F-550°F. If using a pizza stone or cast-iron skillet, place it inside the oven to preheat for at least 20 minutes. ﻿﻿﻿Shape the Dough: Place your pizza dough on a floured surface and gently stretch or roll it into a 12-inch round base. Transfer it to parchment paper, a pizza peel, or a greased pan. ﻿﻿﻿Add the Sauce: Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the center, leaving a small z-inch to 1-inch border around the edge for the crust. ﻿﻿﻿Layer the Cheese and Pepperoni: Cover the sauce with an even layer of shredded mozzarella cheese. Arrange the pepperoni slices on top, slightly overlapping them so every slice gets plenty of coverage. ﻿﻿﻿Bake: Bake for 8 to 12 minutes until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly and slightly charred. ﻿﻿﻿Slice and Serve: Brush the outer crust with a little olive oil or melted garlic butter, let the pizza cool for 3 to 5 minutes, then you take it.

    . After you take it out of the oven you want to add some seasoning such as salt and pepper and then your pizza is ready to eat.
